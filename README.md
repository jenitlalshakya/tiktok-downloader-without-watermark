A simple and fast web application to download **TikTok videos and images without watermark**.  
Just paste the link, and get your content instantly! Powered by **Node.js + Express backend**.

---

## 🚀 Features

- ✅ Download **TikTok videos without watermark**  
- ✅ Download **TikTok image posts (photo sets)**  
- ✅ Auto-generate **custom folder names** (e.g., `@username_video/img_id`)  
- ✅ Works seamlessly on both desktop and mobile browsers  
- ✅ Clean, responsive, and user-friendly interface  
- ✅ Fast and reliable performance with **backend processing for downloads**  

---

## 🆕 Recent Updates

- ✨ Added **organized saving structure**: media is now saved as `@username_video/img_id.mp4/jpeg`  
- ⚡ Improved **mobile compatibility** and **download speed**  
- 🧭 Enhanced **error handling** for invalid, private, or unavailable TikTok URLs  
- 🧩 Optimized **backend and frontend integration** for smoother downloads  

---

## 📥 How to Use

1. Open the **[Live Demo](https://tiktok-downloader-without-watermark.onrender.com/)**  
2. Copy the **TikTok video or image post link** you want to download  
3. Paste the link into the input box  
4. Click the **Download** button  
5. Your video or image(s) will be saved directly to your device in a structured folder
